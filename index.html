Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers to common questions about non-surgical hair patches.

A non-surgical hair patch is a customized hair restoration system made with 100% natural human hair hand-tied to a micro-thin, breathable base (such as Swiss lace or ultra-thin poly skin). It is secured safely to thinning or bald scalp areas using medical-grade dermatologically tested adhesives or clips, without any surgical intervention.
