HOW IT WORKS

What Exactly Is a Non-Surgical Hair Patch?

A modern hair patch (also known as a hair system or cranial prosthesis) is an advanced aesthetic solution designed to cover areas of thinning or baldness with absolute realism.

Unlike old-fashioned synthetic wigs, modern hair patches feature individual strands of real human hair knotted into an ultra-thin membrane. This membrane is custom-contoured to fit your scalp and secured with hypoallergenic, medical-grade bonding agents or clips.

Once fitted and styled by our master technicians, it behaves just like your biological hair: you can shower, style it with products, work out, and live your daily routine without self-consciousness.

BASE TECHNOLOGY

The Engineering Behind Natural Comfort

The foundation of every undetectable hair system is its base material.

Swiss & French Lace

The gold standard in breathability. Porous honeycomb mesh that allows air and sweat to pass freely while creating an invisible front hairline.

Ultra-Thin Poly Skin

0.03mm to 0.06mm polyurethane membrane that hugs the scalp like a second layer of skin. Extremely easy to clean and re-bond.

Mono Filament

Finely woven durable base capable of holding higher hair density. Ideal for active lifestyles and extended durability.

Lace-Skin Hybrid

Combines an undetectable Swiss lace front with a durable poly perimeter for the best balance of realism and easy maintenance.

THE ART OF REALISM

An Undetectable Frontal Hairline

The true test of any hair restoration system is the front hairline. A straight, abrupt wall of hair looks artificial immediately.

At JAZ Hair Studio, we utilize graduated single-knot hairline placement with bleached knots that mirror how hair naturally grows from the scalp: lighter, finer baby hairs at the outermost edge transitioning smoothly into denser hair toward the crown.

Whether you comb your hair forward with texture, slick it back, or part it to the side, your hairline appears authentic from any angle, even in direct sunlight.
