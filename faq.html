CATEGORY 01

General Questions

A non-surgical hair patch is a customized cranial system made with 100% genuine human hair tied to a micro-fine, breathable base (such as Swiss lace or ultra-thin poly skin). It is secured safely to areas of thinning or hair loss using dermatologically approved adhesives or clips, without any surgical incisions.

Yes, completely different. Traditional wigs are mass-produced, heavy, cover the entire head, and often use synthetic hair. A hair patch is custom-crafted to fit only your specific thinning area, uses 100% human hair, matches your exact biological hair texture and color, and is bonded securely for weeks of continuous wear.

When fitted and styled by our master specialists, the hairline is virtually undetectable. We use graduated hairline knots and ultra-thin lace that melts into your scalp, making it look as though the hair is growing directly out of your head.

Yes. Our freestyle hair systems allow parting in any direction: side part, pompadour, back-sweep, textured crop, or messy fringe. Our barber texturizes the hair to your exact preference during the fitting session.

With proper maintenance and gentle care, a premium human hair system typically lasts between 6 to 12 months depending on the base type (ultra-thin skin bases are lighter and replaced sooner, while mono filament bases last longer).

CATEGORY 02

Fitting & Customization

First, we assess your scalp and trim any excess thinning hair within the attachment perimeter. Next, a scalp skin protector is applied, followed by medical adhesive or hypoallergenic tape. The system is placed with contour precision, and finally, our master barber cuts, blends, and styles it seamlessly with your natural hair.

A complete initial consultation, custom fitting, and styling session typically takes approximately 60 to 90 minutes. Monthly maintenance visits take only 45 minutes.

No. The process is completely painless and non-invasive. We use dermatologically tested, latex-free medical bonding agents that are safe for sensitive scalps.

CATEGORY 03

Maintenance & Lifestyle

We recommend a professional servicing every 3 to 4 weeks. This allows us to cleanse your scalp, trim your biological side hair, deep-condition the system, and apply fresh bonding adhesive.

Yes. Our medical-grade adhesives are waterproof and sweat-resistant. You can shower, swim, work out at the gym, and sleep with the hair system attached.

Yes, because it is 100% human hair, our stylists can trim, texture, or adjust the parting during any of your maintenance appointments.
