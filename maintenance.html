DAILY ESSENTIALS

The 3 Pillars of Hair System Longevity

1. Daily Detangling

Always brush your system gently using a specialized loop brush or wide-tooth comb. Begin at the ends and work upward toward the base to prevent pulling on knots.

2. Washing & Conditioning

Wash 2–3 times per week with sulfate-free, alcohol-free shampoo in cool or lukewarm water. Smooth shampoo downward without vigorous circular scrubbing.

3. Matte Styling & Heat

Use lightweight water-based pomades or matte styling clays. If blow-drying, use the cool or medium heat setting to preserve hair moisture and lace integrity.

4-WEEK CYCLE

Your Recommended Maintenance Timeline

EXPERT PROTOCOLS

8 Essential Home Maintenance Tips

1. Sulfate-Free Shampoos
Sulfates strip natural oils from human hair systems. Always use gentle salon-grade formulations.
2. Loop Brush Only
Standard ball-tipped brushes snag on delicate lace knots. A loop brush glides through without tearing.
3. Avoid Hot Water
Hot water can soften adhesive tapes and dry out hair strands. Always wash with cool or lukewarm water.
4. Pat Dry Gently
Never rub vigorously with a towel. Gently blot or press moisture out with a microfiber towel.
5. Leave-In Conditioner
Because system hair doesn't receive natural scalp sebum, daily light leave-in hydration prevents tangling.
6. Silk or Satin Pillowcase
Reduces night friction, preventing tangles and extending the system's smooth texture significantly.
7. Avoid Heavy Oils on Base
Do not apply hair oils directly onto the lace base, as oils accelerate adhesive breakdown.
8. Timely Servicing
Do not delay professional servicing beyond 4–5 weeks to maintain optimal scalp skin hygiene.
